# Introdução à Física (Introduction to Physics)
Repository for the "Introdução à Física" course at Instituto Politécnico da Guarda (IPG), part of the "Licenciatura em Engenharia Informática" degree program. This repository contains exercises, resources, and materials related to the study of Introduction to Physics.


## Course Objectives
Upon completion of this course, students should be able to:
To understand fundamental concepts and principles of Physics (mechanics and electromagnetism) in a way that enables comprehension of topics covered in other course units and their application, namely:
1. Identify fundamental quantities. Describe uniform linear motion, uniformly accelerated motion, and variable motion. Describe movements in two and three dimensions. State the laws of dynamics and apply them to simple situations. Define work and power. State fundamental energy theorems.
2. Define Coulomb’s law and the electric field. Calculate it in simple situations. Define potential and electric potential energy. Define magnetic field and Biot-Savart’s law. Calculate the direction of the force exerted on a conductor carrying a current moving in a magnetic field.
3. Calculate currents and voltages in electrical circuits using Ohm’s and Kirchhoff’s laws. Calculate equivalent models of resistances and capacitors in series or parallel. State Joule’s law.

## Program Contents
### Introduction
- Physical Quantities; International System of Units and dimensions; Scientific notation; Basics of vector and integral calculus.

### Particle Mechanics
- **Kinematics:** Velocity, acceleration, and their components. Characterization of one-dimensional, two-dimensional, and three-dimensional movements. Projectile motion; Circular motion.
- **Dynamics:** Newton’s laws and their relation to motion; Linear momentum and its conservation.
- **Work and Energy:** Work of a force. Power. Kinetic energy theorem and conservation of mechanical energy.

### Electromagnetism
- **Electrostatic Field:** Electric field of a point charge. Coulomb’s law. Work and electric potential energy. Electric potential.
- **Magnetic Field:** Biot-Savart’s law; Electromagnetic interaction; Motion of electric charges in static magnetic fields.

### Direct Current
- Electric current intensity. Ohm’s law. Joule effect; Association of resistances and capacitors in series and parallel. Kirchhoff’s laws.

## Assessment Criteria
The teaching methods and techniques applied during the sessions will include: expository lessons, problem-solving, simulations, and practical laboratory work conducted in groups.
- **Continuous assessment:** Three tests (75%) and practical work (25%).
- **Final assessment:** Exam (100%) or Exam (75%) and practical work (25%). Practical work must be completed on the proposed dates during the course.
